Signature Domestic Tour

7 Days Enchanting Kashmir &
Gulmarg Alpine Odyssey

⏱ Duration: 6 Nights / 7 Days
📍 Route: Srinagar • Gulmarg • Pahalgam • Sonamarg
🏨 Stays: 4★/5★ Hotels & Luxury Houseboat
🚗 Transport: Dedicated Private AC SUV

Trip Highlights

  • ✦ 1 Night in Handcrafted Nigeen Lake Houseboat
  • ✦ Gondola Cable Car Ride in Gulmarg
  • ✦ Private Shikara Sunset Ride on Dal Lake
  • ✦ Lidder River Valley Walk & Betaab Valley
  • ✦ Thajiwas Glacier Sledge in Sonamarg
  • ✦ Mughal Gardens (Nishat & Shalimar Bagh)

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Arrive at Srinagar Airport where our private chauffeur welcomes you. Transfer to your luxury cedar houseboat. In the evening, enjoy a 1-hour relaxing private Shikara ride on Dal Lake passing floating vegetable markets. Overnight in houseboat with dinner included.
Drive through scenic willow and pine forests to Gulmarg (2,730m). Board the famous Gulmarg Gondola to Phase 1 (Kongdoori) and Phase 2 (Apharwat Peak) for panoramic Himalayan views. Enjoy snow activities or golf. Overnight in Gulmarg resort.
Drive past Pampore saffron fields and Awantipora temple ruins to Pahalgam. Check into your riverside resort along the crystal-clear Lidder River. Relax amidst pine aroma and mountain air. Overnight in Pahalgam.
Take a local excursion to Betaab Valley, Aru Valley, and Chandanwari. Walk through meadows, sip fresh Kashmiri Kahwa tea, and click postcard photos against snow-capped peaks. Overnight in Pahalgam.
Return to Srinagar. Visit the terraced Mughal Gardens: Nishat Bagh (Garden of Pleasure), Shalimar Bagh (Abode of Love), and Chashme Shahi. Visit the Shankaracharya Temple atop Gopadari Hill. Overnight in Srinagar hotel.
Full day trip along the Sindh River to Sonamarg (2,800m). Enjoy pony rides to Thajiwas Glacier where snow remains year-round. Return to Srinagar for evening handicraft shopping (Pashmina shawls, saffron, walnut woodcraft). Overnight in Srinagar.
Enjoy a leisurely breakfast overlooking the mountains. Transfer to Srinagar Airport with unforgettable memories of paradise.
