6 Days Grand Dubai &
Abu Dhabi Luxury Odyssey

⏱ Duration: 5 Nights / 6 Days
📍 Route: Downtown Dubai • Desert Dunes • Abu Dhabi • Marina
🏨 Stays: 4★/5★ Premium City & Marina Hotels
🚗 Transport: Dedicated Private Luxury AC SUV

Trip Highlights

  • ✦ Burj Khalifa 124th & 125th Floor Observation Deck
  • ✦ 4x4 Red Dune Desert Safari with BBQ & Live Shows
  • ✦ Luxury Dubai Marina 5-Star Dhow Dinner Cruise
  • ✦ Abu Dhabi Tour: Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque & Louvre
  • ✦ Future Museum & Dubai Frame Photo-Stop
  • ✦ Palm Jumeirah Monorail & Atlantis The Palm View
  • ✦ Dubai Mall & World-Famous Fountain Show
  • ✦ Historic Creek Abra Ride, Gold & Spice Souks

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Arrive at Dubai International Airport (DXB) where our private chauffeur welcomes you with personalized assistance. Transfer to your luxury hotel in a private air-conditioned vehicle. In the evening, head to the world-famous Dubai Marina for a 2-hour luxury glass dhow cruise with an international 5-star buffet dinner, live music, and mesmerizing views of illuminated skyscrapers. Overnight in Dubai.
Enjoy breakfast before embarking on a guided city tour covering Zabeel Palace, the towering Dubai Frame, Jumeirah Mosque, and a scenic photo-stop at the sail-shaped Burj Al Arab. In the afternoon, visit Dubai Mall and ascend to the 124th & 125th floors of Burj Khalifa—the world’s tallest tower—for breathtaking 360-degree panoramic vistas. End the evening watching the synchronized Dubai Fountain show. Overnight in Dubai.
Morning at leisure to relax or explore nearby cafes. In the afternoon, embark on an exhilarating 4x4 Land Cruiser safari across the Lahbab red dunes. Experience high-octane dune bashing, sandboarding, and sunset photo opportunities. Arrive at a luxury Bedouin desert camp for camel riding, henna painting, fire dancers, Tanoura shows, and a lavish Arabian BBQ buffet dinner under the stars. Overnight in Dubai.
Take a scenic private drive to the capital city of UAE, Abu Dhabi. Tour the breathtaking Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, a masterpiece of pure white marble, crystal chandeliers, and handcrafted carpets. Continue along the picturesque Abu Dhabi Corniche, view Emirates Palace and the presidential palace Qasr Al Watan, and visit the iconic Louvre Abu Dhabi museum or photo-stop at Ferrari World on Yas Island. Return to Dubai in the evening. Overnight in Dubai.
Visit the architectural triumph — Museum of the Future. Next, ride the scenic Palm Monorail overlooking the man-made Palm Jumeirah archipelago, concluding at the magnificent Atlantis The Palm. Spend your afternoon at Aquaventure Waterpark, The Lost Chambers Aquarium, or stroll along The Pointe and JBR beach promenade. Overnight in Dubai.
After a leisurely breakfast, check out and journey into historical Old Dubai. Ride an authentic wooden Abra boat across Dubai Creek to explore the Gold Souk and fragrant Spice Souk for saffron, dates, and perfumes. Finally, our chauffeur will transfer you to Dubai International Airport for your return flight with unforgettable memories of the Emirates.
