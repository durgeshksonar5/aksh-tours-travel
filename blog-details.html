The Ultimate First-Timer’s Guide to Visiting Kashmir in 2026

✍️ Written by Aksh Destination Desk 📅 Published: September 2026 ⏱ 6 Min Read

"If there is a heaven on earth, it is here, it is here, it is here." — Mughal Emperor Jahangir’s centuries-old words still ring true every time the morning mist lifts over Dal Lake.

Whether you are planning a romantic honeymoon, an active family mountain holiday, or a serene getaway, Kashmir requires thoughtful advance planning. In this guide, our destination curators outline the insider secrets for an unforgettable trip.

1. Choosing the Right Season

Kashmir changes completely across seasons. December to February offers thick snow landscapes in Gulmarg for skiing and winter lovers. April and May bring the blooming of Asia's largest Tulip Garden in Srinagar along with mild spring weather. October and November bring the golden chinar autumn foliage, making it a photographer's paradise.

2. Houseboat Stays: What Nobody Tells You

While Dal Lake is lively and close to city bazaars, Nigeen Lake offers cleaner waters, serene birdlife, and a far more luxurious, peaceful ambiance. When booking with Aksh Tours, we prioritize handcrafted cedar wood houseboats equipped with authentic walnut wood carving and modern heating facilities.

3. Securing Gulmarg Gondola Tickets in Advance

Gulmarg Gondola is the world’s second-highest operating cable car. Tickets for Phase 2 (Apharwat Peak at 13,780 ft) sell out weeks in advance during peak season. Our team pre-books your verified slots so you never have to stand in chaotic queues.
